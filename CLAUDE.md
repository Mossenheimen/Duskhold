# 12_Duskhold

An open-world fantasy survival RPG in Project Zomboid's style. The game comes first. Later it becomes a fast world for the PPO agent of `11_PPO-Zombie`, for training and maybe NPC AI.

- `CONCEPT.md`: what the game is. Decisions go in its "Decided" section.
- `ARCHITECTURE.md`: how it's built, the build order in short, and where the setup stands. Open questions are at the end; answered ones move into the text.
- `HISTORY.md`: the build log: each step and slice, what it built, how it was checked, what it left. A new slice adds its entry at the end of its step, with its "not yet".
- `BACKLOG.md`: what's still to build, ranked, in one list. A slice's "not yet" goes here too; what's built comes off it.
- `VISUALS.md`: how it looks: the camera, light, the colour tokens, shapes, surfaces, the drawn senses, type, the GUI's layout and parts, and how the AI and people work on the look. Read it before changing anything on screen, and change it in the same commit as the look.
- `crates/dusk-core/`: the simulation (no dependencies, no graphics, no files, no threads), a file a system:
  - `creature.rs` holds what a kind of creature is, and what it takes of a blow by weapon (`resist`: bone turns a blade) (a row of numbers in `Defs::creatures`; an actor of it is `ActorKind::Creature(n)`);
  - `combat.rs` holds the one rule for timed actions and the fight (the swing, the heavy swing (`Heavy`): a long tell for more harm and a knock-down, the shove and its knock-back; going after one of the dead, a click on it, Diablo's: `Intent::Pursue`, kept and walked after by `act` and `walk_toward` until it's in reach, then one swing);
  - `world.rs` holds the tick, and movement: the dead's steps tile to tile, a character's glide (`World::glide`: a little each tick, anywhere in its tile, `Actor::offset`, sliding along walls; `footstep` a tile walked), and `walk_toward` (a click's way, or a spell walked into reach);
  - `edge.rs` each class of weapon's own edge (`Edges`, rules.toml's `[edges]`, by the weapon's skill, a character's only): a blade's more harm on one that's down, a blunt blow's knock-down, an axe's bleeding (`World::bleed`; the dead bleed out in `World::bleed_out`, bone not at all: `Creature::bleeds`), a spear's drive back a tile (`World::drive_back`, the shove's `knock_back`); on the weapon's card after its numbers;
  - `grab.rs` engagement, and the stagger: a blow of the dead's that lands slows a character a moment (`Actor::staggered`, a tick; rules.toml's `[stagger]`; shorter by a class's and a talent's `staggered`, `World::stagger_ticks`), read in `rare.rs`'s `pace`; and grabs, struggling and the drag-down, **off in the game** since the second playtest (`[grab]`'s chances are 0), kept and tested for a kind that might hold later (`Rules::default()` still grabs);
  - `items.rs` items, containers that fill from the seed, and the item actions;
  - `body.rs` health, endurance and wounds;
  - `needs.rs` the clock, hunger, thirst and fatigue, sleep, healing and moodles;
  - `light.rs` day and night, each tile's light, and the torches and candles held up;
  - `view.rs` the character's field of view and the fog of war;
  - `skills.rs` skills that grow by real use under risk, repeats that pay less, the free cap and masteries, and what a level does;
  - `barricade.rs` boarding up doors and windows, prying boards off, the boards the dead beat through before the door, and repairing (`World::can_repair`: a beaten board re-nailed, then a beaten door, `World::door_beaten`);
  - `build.rs` doors and walls of your own: made from planks and nails (recipes), then put up with a hammer on the side you face (`ItemKind::Piece`, `World::can_put_up`): a wall of planks (`Edge::PlankWall`: stops walking and sight, the dead beat it down as a door, mended as a door) on any open edge, a door only in a gap framed at both ends (`Edge::frames`);
  - `home.rs` home (where you slept the night through), the stash, finds that pay their worth once, and character levels;
  - `fever.rs` ghoul fever: caught from a wound, three stages a game day each, and the cure;
  - `remains.rs` what becomes of a body: getting up again, burning it (oil and a flame), fire through a pile, ash;
  - `injury.rs` wounds by where they land (worked out from the wounds: arms swing slower and less surely, legs walk slower and run dearer, the head aims worse and sees less far, the torso hurts more);
  - `dread.rs` pain (worked out from the wounds: more breath, no sleep in agony) and dread (kept: rising toward what the dark, the dead near, pain and hunger are worth; shaky aim, and frightened, no step nearer the dead);
  - `hunting.rs` beasts that flee (`Mind::Flee`) and wild hunters (wolves: `wild`, the dead's mind), neither counted as the dead, and butchering;
  - `cooking.rs` cooking at a fire, and food that goes off by the hour (unopened containers aged with the world);
  - `archery.rs` bows: an arrow along the line to the first one on it, walls stopping it;
  - `shield.rs` shields on the off arm, bracing to block, the bash;
  - `armour.rs` armour worn a piece to a place on the body: blows that glance off (no wound, a bruise), what it costs (weight, breath, noise, sight), the dead in armour and what gets through it;
  - `climb.rs` climbing through an open window or over a fence (walking straight into it): a step in its own ticks, costing breath as running does, and the dead that climb by their kind, only after something, some tumbling in (`path::find_way` is the search that climbs); and windows' glass: beaten out by the dead that climb (or your blow), broken for good, cutting whoever climbs through;
  - `spell.rs` spells: data (`spells.toml`), casting as a timed action (words that a blow sets back, `Rules::spell_setback`, rules.toml's `[casting]`; cast on a spot too far or out of sight, a character walks closer first: `combat.rs`'s `act` keeps the intent, `world.rs`'s `walk_toward` walks it), a Wizard's mana (`World::max_mana`, `World::mana_regen`: a stat raised by Arcana, talents and gear's `Boosts`, coming back each tick), Arcana, and the six: the fight's first (Firebolt, `SpellKind::Bolt`: fire flies the line to the spot, the first one on it burns; Fireball: a burst, burning what it puts down; Fire Nova, `SpellKind::Nova`: round you, thrown back a tile; what fire does to each is `scorch`'s), then Light (a lure at night), Silence (no sound made), Fog (the dead can't see in or out);
  - `guard.rs` on guard, Zomboid's combat stance (the right mouse button held): facing where it's turned (`World::face`, the cursor), not where it walks, slower (`Rules::guard_pace`), no running; a swing goes where it faces (`combat.rs`'s `aim`); and being in a fight (`World::in_combat`: held, or seen by one of the dead within `IN_COMBAT` tiles), out of which taking a thing in hand or putting armour on or off is done at once (`World::equip_ticks`);
  - `stealth.rs` sneaking (a stance: slower, quieter, seen only from nearer), the sneak attack and Stealth;
  - `bag.rs` pockets and the bag on your back: room in their grids (taking is bounded; what comes otherwise and doesn't fit falls at your feet, `Event::NoRoom`), what's in a bag weighing less and slower to reach, moving a stack across (onto a cell), and a bag dropped full (`World::held`) and taken up again, each thing where it was;
  - `pack.rs` Path of Exile's grids: the pockets, a bag and each container so many cells across and down (`Cells`), each item its size (`Defs::size`), a stack on its top-left cell (`Item::cell`); placing (`place`: where asked, onto stacks alike, then the first free place down each column), arranging within a grid at once (`World::arrange`, `arrange_store`), and an older save's pack and containers laid out as it loads (`World::settle`);
  - `cold.rs` wet and cold: the rain and the shallows soaking you, drying under a roof and by a fire, the cold drifting toward what the night, the wet and the rain are worth (less a roof, a bed, armour's warmth), shivering and freezing;
  - `fitness.rs` Strength and Fitness: slow stats from 0 to 10, the day's breath spent and strain (blows, shoves, hauling) turning into growth at midnight, the more the less each, and what each does (carry, harm, a shove's knock-down; cheaper breath, quicker back);
  - `craft.rs` crafting: recipes from what you carry with a tool to hand, and furniture broken down for planks;
  - `fire.rs` a fire for your base: a hearth, stove or forge laid with fuel (what it holds itself first, then your pack's), lit from a light held up, burning down, put out, lighting its room, and drawing the dead that see it at night;
  - `decoy.rs` decoys: a clockwork thing (`ItemKind::Decoy`) wound and set down where you stand, rattling on a beat from when it's set to start till it runs down (`World::decoys`, saved), the dead that hear it going to it as to any sound; taken up, it stops;
  - `potion.rs` potions and the belt: potions as data (`items/potions.toml`, `ItemKind::Potion`), drunk as anything's used and working a while (`Actor::doses`: life and mana back a share a tick; haste, iron skin, vigour and courage for their time, each read where its rule is: the glide, `strike`, `endurance_rate`, `minds`; a moodle each, `Event::Waned` at the end); the belt (`Item::belt`, rules.toml's `[belt]`): a place of its own in the pack's list, out of the pockets' room, where a potion that comes to you goes first (`carry_in`, `belt_in`), and quickest to drink from (`drink_ticks`);
  - `pressure.rs` the region's pressure (time and the risen raise it, burning lowers it) and the dead walking in where you aren't;
  - `holdout.rs` the holdouts: hallowed ground (the dead never come in, nor heed you there), and the counter (buy from a daily stock, sell for coin, supply it, mend);
  - `tolling.rs` the Tolling: the region's calendar, the bell, the dead drawn to it and not giving up till dawn;
  - `flow.rs` flow fields, one Dijkstra map shared by a crowd going to one place;
  - `class.rs` the classes (the Fighter, the Cleric and the Wizard), choosing one before you're under way, a class's kit, and talents: three offered a level, one picked, each changing numbers the rules already use, Cleave and Second Wind adding a little;
  - `faith.rs` the Cleric's faith: prayer at a shrine, turning the dead (`Mind::Turned`: they flee, and a blow ends it), consecration (holy water or faith: `Remains::Hallowed`), Mending, wards on doors, Steady and Last Rites;
  - `forage.rs` wild plants (`Defs::plants`), picking one (E), plants growing back (`World::regrowing`) and Foraging, the remedies being items' own (a poultice is a bandage with a `salve`, a tea a drink with `comfort`);
  - `weather.rs` rain: spells from the seed and the clock, lights put out, sound and scent carrying less outdoors, a darker sky;
  - `magic.rs` magic items: rarity, properties and curses as data, unknown until read or learnt by use, a cursed weapon clinging; a weapon's numbers as made and as far as you know its magic (`Defs::made_swing`, `Defs::card`); what a weapon or bow in hand, a shield on the arm and armour worn give their bearer (`Boosts`: Strength, Fitness, levels in skills, read by `World::skill_level` and `fitness.rs`); armour as made and as its magic changes it (`Defs::made_armour`: quality's turning, ward, warmth, steps, wear; a piece known once it's put on); a bow and a shield as made and as their magic changes them (`Defs::made_bow`: harm, draw, aim, range, arrows broken; `Defs::made_guard`: block, bash, breath, wear; a bow learnt by shots that land, a shield known once it's taken up); properties by what they go on (`Fits`: a weapon, a light, armour, a bow, a shield: each its own, Path of Exile's mods by item class) and its blows (fire and holy harm: `World::elemental`); the uniques (weapons, armour, bows and shields made by hand, each with an effect of its own, its kind's: `Gifts`, read as a blow or a shot begins, as a block is made (`World::gifted_block`), and from what's worn as a blow lands on you (`World::thorns`); a piece that calms slows dread's rise, `World::worn_calm`); Rares as Path of Exile's (three or four properties, a made-up name: `Defs::thing_name`); properties in families of tiers, the best tier a find rolls set by how far out it was found (`World::find_level`), and where its numbers fall in that tier's range (`Magic::rolls`, `Property::rolled`, `Defs::rolls_of`; rules.toml's `[magic] spread`);
  - `card.rs` a thing's card, as Path of Exile's and Diablo's (`World::card`): its name and kind, its numbers (marked where its making or magic changed them), its making and its properties as numbers with their words and tiers, a unique's effect and story in words, and notes (unknown, against yours, a find); `CardLine`s the client draws by their `CardStyle`;
  - `quality.rs` a weapon's, a bow's, a shield's or a piece of armour's quality: crude, plain, fine or masterwork, rolled as it's found, changing a weapon's or a bow's harm, armour's turning and a shield's block, its wear and its worth;
  - `rare.rs` rare monsters: affixes on top of a kind's numbers (`World::stats`), names, packs of finds, the Lantern-Eater;
  - `legacy.rs` death and legacy: names, the body rising at midnight as a revenant in its gear, gear falling where a creature dies, the next character;
  - `save.rs` (behind the `save` feature, the core's only dependencies: serde and ciborium) a world as bytes: the seed plus what changed, in CBOR so it outlasts updates (fields by name; the data's numbers put onto the data's own by id; a chunk as its kinds of tile and edge and runs of them, `grid.rs`'s `SavedChunk`).
- `crates/dusk-data/`: loads and checks `data/` (serde + toml), turning a designer's units into the core's; and the looks (`looks.rs`: `looks.toml`'s materials and every look as the kit's parts, a fixture's and an item's, with the corners the client makes, for the client and the tools; the core never reads them).
- `crates/dusk-gen/`: a patch of a region from a seed: the layout (road, the market town's lots where there's room for the rest, the holdout, the river, the farms out in the fields), then every tile as a pure function, then the dead and the rares.
- `crates/dusk-cli/`: the demo, the benchmark, the data and world tools, and the fight simulator (`fight.rs`: the game's data, the dead against a class, many times over, for tuning).
- `crates/dusk-godot/`: the Godot extension (gdext). Its `DuskWorld` node makes a patch, ticks it, and hands GDScript the map a chunk at a time, the actors, what the dead can see, noises and events. It takes intents back.
- `data/`: the game's numbers and content in TOML: `rules.toml` (with `[casting]`, how far a blow sets a spell's words back, `[stagger]`, a blow's brief slow, `[grab]`, off, `[edges]`, each class of weapon's own, `[building]`, doors and walls of your own, `[belt]`, the belt of potions, `[faith]`, the Cleric's, `[foraging]`, `[cold]`, and `[quality]`, how well weapons, bows, shields and armour found were made), `creatures/`, `fixtures.toml` (a bed is two tiles, `bed` its head and `bed_foot`, `B` in the prefabs, the tile on from it, its look's `backs` turning it to its head; an altar is a `shrine`; a wild plant has `forage`), `holdouts.toml` (the holdouts' trade, and which item is coin), `recipes.toml` (what can be made), `names.toml` (characters' and rares' names, and `[things]`: words for Rare things' made-up names), `affixes.toml` (rares' affixes), `properties.toml` (magic items' properties and curses), `uniques.toml` (weapons, armour, bows and shields made by hand, each with its own effect), `classes.toml` and `talents.toml` (the Fighter, the Cleric and the Wizard, their kits and talents), `spells.toml` (the Wizard's spells), `looks.toml` (how everything looks: the world's materials, every fixture made of the kit's parts, what's strewn on the ground, and every item, its icon drawn from it, `VISUALS.md` §5), `items/`, `loot/`, `prefabs/` (buildings as text grids; `town.toml` the shops, `farm.toml` the farmsteads, `graveyard.toml` the graveyard and its skeletons, `barrow.toml` the barrows and their wights, `abbey.toml` the holdout; the chapel, with its fonts of holy water, is in `hamlet.toml`), `regions/`.
- `client/`: the Godot 4.7 project, built in code. It draws what the core says and decides nothing:
  - `main.gd`: input, the camera and the loop; the window (the player's game maximised, a trial run 1600 × 900);
  - `codes.gd`: the numbers DuskWorld hands over, by name (`Event.HEARD`, `Item.BOW`, `Action.SWING`, ...). Made from `crates/dusk-godot/src/codes.rs` by its test, never by hand: after changing a code, run `cargo test -p dusk-godot codes` with `DUSK_WRITE_CODES=1`;
  - `book_panel.gd`: what the book panels share (skills, making, spells): the panel at the left middle, opening and closing, and drawing only when what they show changes;
  - `settings.gd`: the settings screen (F10): the GUI's size, colour-blind mode, high contrast, fewer flashes, numbers on the bars, the sound rings and what the dead see on the ground (both off unless asked for), and remapping;
  - `art.gd`: the GUI's dress, as Diablo's and Path of Exile's (`VISUALS.md` §4): what panels (a Frame), cells and slots (a Well), buttons and a card's title (a Plate), a thing's tile (a Glow), bars (a Fill) and rules are drawn as, from SVG in `icons/ui/`; every panel takes it through `Look.panel_style`. The globes either side of the bar are `shaders/globe.gdshader`, drawn by `hotbar.gd`;
  - `menu.gd`: Esc's menu, with nothing else open to close: Resume, Settings, Save and quit, the world waiting under it; the keys' defaults (`KEYS`, laid out in `VISUALS.md` §10, "The keys"); all kept in `user://settings.cfg` by the player's own game, with the GUI's size (Ctrl+- and Ctrl+=), the zoom it was left at (the wheel, `-` and `=`), and the bar's order of powers, by class (`hotbar.gd`);
  - `look.gd`: the look's tokens, `VISUALS.md` in code: every colour, the text sizes and their faces (`sized`), and the GUI's parts (`panel_style`, `text`, `bar`, `tile_style`). Every script that draws preloads it, and uses a token, never a colour of its own. The world's colours (the ground's, the buildings', a bundle on a floor) are `looks.toml`'s materials, taken in as the looks are read (`take_materials`). The settings that change the look live there too (`colour_blind`, `high_contrast`, `reduce_motion`, `show_numbers`): a token one changes (the alert states, `PANEL`) is a static var, and whatever copies it copies it again when `Look.version` moves on;
  - `looks.gd`: the kit (`VISUALS.md` §5): `DuskWorld.looks()` read once, each look's variant baked into one flat-shaded mesh (its parts' colours and surfaces in the vertices) that everything drawn of it shares; an item's too (`item_mesh`);
  - `icons.gd`: each item's icon drawn from its look, on a small stage of its own (orthographic, from the front and a little above), fitted to its cells, once, and kept; none with no window;
  - `gallery.tscn` (`gallery.gd`): every look laid out under the game's camera and light, a picture route of its own (`VISUALS.md` §14), or with `--icons` every item's icon; no world is made;
  - `fonts/`: Alegreya Sans and Alegreya (SIL OFL, with their licences and a README). The sans is the project's default font; their `.import` files turn on lining, tabular figures, so commit them. `play.cmd` imports any font not yet imported;
  - `icons/`: white SVG silhouettes on a 64 px canvas (`moodles/`: one for each moodle, named in `hud.gd`'s `MOODLES`; `powers/`: the bar's spells and powers; `potion.svg`: a flask, inked in what the potion does), inked as they're drawn. `project.godot`'s `[importer_defaults]` imports textures at twice their canvas with mipmaps; commit the `.import` files. `play.cmd` imports any icon not yet imported;
  - `map_view.gd`: streams chunks round the camera, and draws the fog of war and the sight-cone overlay;
  - `chunk_view.gd`: one chunk's ground, walls and fixtures, each fixture its look turned to fit its tile (`looks.toml`'s `placed`, `faces`, `backs`; `DuskWorld.chunk_walls()`), and what's strewn on its ground (`looks.toml`'s `[[scatter]]`);
  - `numbers.gd` (damage numbers: each blow's harm rising where it landed, red on you), `bars.gd` (health bars over the dead you see that are hurt, and over rares);
  - `actor_view.gd` (poses: the lean of a wind-up, a heavy swing rearing back, kneeling to pray, bending to pick, a power's glow, lying down, dead), `effects.gd` (a power, a spell or a blow as it happens: motes, waves, a fireball's flight, blood and sparks; `tests/check_effects.gd` makes each once for `check.cmd`), `tells.gd` (a swing's tell on the ground), `threats.gd` (engagement lines), `bag.gd` (what's within reach as a list while the inventory is shut, and the rules for what a thing's buttons do, shared with the inventory: `use`, `take`, `buy`, `mend`, `read`), `inventory.gd` (I or Tab: the inventory as Path of Exile 2's: what you hold and wear round a figure, the belt, the pockets' and the bag's grids; containers, the ground and a counter as tabs on the left; click to pick up and put down where the core's checks say it fits, right-click to use, Ctrl-click to move across; a thing from a container or the ground put on the figure is taken, then put on as it lands; a thing's card on hover, `DuskWorld.card_lines()` drawn by the HUD's detail panel), `skills.gd` (the skills panel; each row's progress to its next level on hover), `craft.gd` (the crafting panel), `spells.gd` (the spells panel), `hotbar.gd` (the bar, bottom centre: your powers on the number keys in your order, kept by class in `settings.cfg`, then the belt's loops on the next keys; a click uses a slot, hovering opens its card, a power dragged onto another swaps them, one new since the order was kept coming first; XP along its top, and breath along its foot while the right globe holds mana or faith), `zones.gd` (the spells lasting on their spots, alive and fading as they end), `aim.gd` (a readied spell's reach, round you and at the mouse), `litter.gd` (what lies on the floor), `noise_rings.gd`, `hud.gd` (clock, body panel, the moodles as tiles with their detail on hover, news, the act's bar and the prompts under you, the sleep veil, death screen), `prompts.gd` (what you can do beside you, worded from the core's checks: E does the likeliest, "[E] Pick the yarrow", and held lists it all; key caps read from the InputMap: `Prompts.keyed`), `body_view.gd` (the body diagram: each part tinted by its worst wound);
  - `shaders/`: the cut-away is `cut_away.gdshaderinc`, also used for the kit's parts (a crown is cut, a trunk isn't). `fog_of_war.gdshader` is a full-screen pass over everything opaque, before the overlays. Every surface is procedural (`surfaces.gdshaderinc`: noise, blocks, boards, and `sf_surface`, every surface by its code), worked out from where a point is: the ground by its kind (in the ground texture's alpha; the texture has a tile of the next chunks' round it, `DuskWorld.chunk_grounds()`, so borders between natural grounds can wander), walls by a surface code in each MultiMesh instance's custom data, and the kit's parts by theirs in UV2 (`looks.gdshader`). Nothing is downloaded.
- `play.cmd`: builds the extension and starts the game.
- `research/`: what's behind the decisions.
  - `game_design.md`: the 2026-11-06 design research (combat, loot, athletics, levelling, pacing and more), with its sources. It was accepted and folded into CONCEPT.md the same day.
  - `controls.md`: the 2026-11-12 look at similar games' keys (Zomboid, Valheim, Don't Starve and others), why the keys were redone, and the layout that came of it, with its sources.
  - The pure-Python benchmarks of a tick and of its hot spots (any Python 3).

## Commands (PowerShell, from this folder)

```powershell
.\play.cmd                                 # build and play: carries on from the saved game (the first run also imports the project)
.\play.cmd -e                              # the same, but open the Godot editor on client/
.\play.cmd gallery.tscn -- --zoom=8 --only=bed,table --shot=PNG --shot-after=2   # the looks gallery (VISUALS.md §14): --only, --zoom, --cols, --daylight=0..100, --torch, --data
.\play.cmd -- --seed=7 --walk=4,5          # a trial run, not saved. Flags after --: --seed, --town, --walk=X,Y, --zoom, --speed, --data=DIR, --class=NAME (a trial run asks for no class: the first, else), --do=S:ACTION (swing, swing_n/_e/_s/_w, heavy, shove, door, struggle, drop, take, dropbag, tobag, spells, cast_light, cast_silence, cast_fog, cast_fireball, cast_firebolt, cast_fire_nova, sleep, drink, light, skills, save, board, unboard, burn, turn, pray, consecrate, ward, mend, pick, wear, wield (the last weapon or bow in the pack: the last given), dropweapon (the same, dropped at your feet), equipin (a thing within reach put on the figure through the inventory, a weapon first: taken, then put on), shield (the first shield in the pack, on the arm), keys, moodle (the worst one's detail), esc (what Esc closes first, else the menu; never quits), run (Shift tapped: walking or running), guard (on guard, facing the nearest of the dead you see), unguard, pursue (after the nearest of the dead still standing, as a click on it), settings (the screen, F10), body (the worst-wounded part's detail), card (a weapon's card in the pack, else a bow's, a shield's or armour's, else the hand's), bandage (the first in the pack), decoy (the first in the pack, wound and set down), repair (the door or window beside you), putup (the first door or wall of planks in the pack, on the side faced), belt1 to belt4 (the belt's keys: the potion in that loop drunk), tobelt (the first potion off the belt, put on it), bar (the bar's slots in words), swap (the bar's first two powers swapped, as a drag does), use (E tapped), here (E held: the list), power1 to power4 (the number keys), next), --news (and the prompts), --look=colour_blind,high_contrast,reduce_motion,show_numbers (settings on, not kept), --aim=DX,DY (a readied spell, and a script's, aimed at your tile plus that: for pictures), --give=ITEM:GRADE:PROP+PROP,... (finds in the pack at the start, made so, their magic known: --give=sword:masterwork:keen; three or four properties make a named Rare: --give=sword::mighty+searing+seasoned; or a unique by its id: --give=redhand), --hour=H, --save=FILE, --choose (a trial run asks who comes), --shot=PNG --shot-after=S
.\check.cmd                                # every check before a commit: fmt, clippy, tests, the data, then the game headless and every effect once (check.cmd rust: the Rust only)
cargo test --workspace                     # 490 tests, a few seconds (DUSK_WRITE_SAVES=1 only to write a new golden save)
cargo run -p dusk-cli -- check             # load and check everything in data/
cargo run -p dusk-cli -- prefab cottage    # a building, in its four turns
cargo run --release -p dusk-cli -- gen --seed 7 --png map.png   # a patch: its lots, its dead by kind, the XP, cures and oil its containers hold, and a map (--text X,Y,W,H, --ticks N: also the pressure after, --tolling: its first Tolling, run)
cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets     # lints; keep it at zero warnings
cargo fmt --all                            # standard formatting
cargo run -p dusk-cli -- demo              # the scene, printed once a second of game time
cargo run --release -p dusk-cli -- bench --dead 100 --think-every 5   # ticks a second (ARCHITECTURE.md, performance budget); --tolling: with a bell drawing them
```

## Conventions

- **Build output** goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\cargo-target` (`CARGO_TARGET_DIR`), never into OneDrive. In the Bash tool, export it, and `PATH=$HOME/.cargo/bin:$PATH`, in each command: its shells don't see `setx` changes.
- **Determinism:**
  - no `HashMap` iteration in game logic (`hash::FastMap` is for lookups only: its hasher, `WordHasher`, is fast and nothing may depend on what it hashes to; `Fnv64` is for hashes that are kept or compared);
  - gameplay randomness only through `hash::roll(seed, tick, who, why)`;
  - actors in id order, and one world per thread.
- **Test maps are text grids** (`text.rs`), the same format as prefabs and `World::render`. Their `z` is creature 0, the test shambler (`Creature::default`).
- **A creature is data, never a rule.** A new kind is a `[[creature]]` in `data/creatures/`; if it needs a number no creature has, add the field to `Creature` (with its conversion and check in `dusk-data`), not to `Rules`. In a test, change a kind with `w.creature_mut(n)`, after any `w.defs = ...`: the numbers live in the definitions.
- **A fixture's look is data too.** A new `[[fixture]]` needs a look in `data/looks.toml`, made of the kit's parts in its materials (`VISUALS.md` §5): the data won't load without one. Check it in the looks gallery before the world. So does a new item (`[[item]]`, at its own size, standing as it lies in its cells: its icon is drawn from it); check it with `gallery.tscn -- --icons`.
- **An affix is data too** (`data/affixes.toml`): what it does to a `Creature`'s numbers (`Affix::apply`). A rule asking what a creature can do asks `World::stats(id)` (its kind's numbers with its affixes on), not `World::creature(kind)`. Three hot paths read the kind in place for speed (the thinking beat, `sees`, `pace` for all but a rare): an affix that changed what they read would need them to ask `stats`.
- **The game's numbers live in `data/`,** in a designer's units (tiles a second, seconds, degrees). `dusk-data` converts them into the core's units and checks every reference: a new field needs its conversion and its check there. `Rules::default()` is a fixed set for tests and tools, not the game's tuning.
- **Saves:**
  - **Saves outlast updates** (format 37 on, CBOR: every field and variant by name). A new field on a saved type needs `#[cfg_attr(feature = "save", serde(default))]` (or `default = "..."` when its type's default isn't right), and no `save::VERSION` change: an older save loads it as its default. `dusk-gen`'s golden save (`tests/saves/v37.dusk`, never rewritten) fails with "missing field" until it has one. A renamed field or variant keeps its old name with `serde(alias)`. A fixed array by an enum is saved by name (`skills.rs`'s `by_skill`). `VERSION` moves only for a change no default or alias carries, with a step reading the older format, and a golden save of the new one beside the old. A new type inside a saved one needs `#[cfg_attr(feature = "save", derive(serde::Serialize, serde::Deserialize))]`.
  - **A save names the data by id:** a field that holds a number into the data (an item, a property, a fixture) is put onto the data's own by `save.rs`'s `Remap` as the save loads, through the table's ids in `Ids` (a new table needs adding to both). Its destructuring of `Actor`, `Item`, `Magic` and the state names every field, so a new field won't compile until it's said whether it names the data.
  - World state added outside the actors and the grid (a new map in `World`, or the pressure) must be added to `save.rs`'s `State` by hand, and to `state_hash`. The save test's "same hash after loading" catches one that's hashed but not saved. What can be worked out from the actors (`World::fires`) isn't saved: `set_actors` works it out again.
  - The region (`World::region`) comes from the data, as the rules do: `dusk-gen`'s `patch` sets it, never a save. So do `World::hallowed` and the Tolling's bell; its field is worked out from the map (`Grid::edits` says when it's stale).
- **Buildings:** a new or changed prefab must leave every floor reachable from its door (furniture blocks corners: a diagonal needs both tiles beside it free). `dusk-gen`'s `every_floor_of_every_building_can_be_walked_to_from_its_door` checks it. Tests find buildings through the layout (`Layout::lots`), never by a seed's coordinates: the town moves when anything in it changes. A grid whose north side is open ground needs `+` corners on its first line: the parser drops blank first and last lines.
  - **Actors are only ever added,** never removed (bodies stay, as ash), so an actor's index in the bridge's arrays never changes. The client adds a view for each newcomer (`_add_new_actors`).
  - Rules and definitions are never saved: they come from `data/`.
  - **Any flag after `--` makes a trial run,** which neither loads nor saves the player's world (`user://world.dusk`). Test saving with `--save=<scratchpad>/x.dusk`.
- **Speed:**
  - a grid built by hand for anything that searches a lot (a tool, a benchmark) calls `Grid::index_steps()` once it's made, as `dusk-gen`'s `patch` does: searches then read a byte a tile. Unindexed, the answers are the same, slower;
  - before making something faster, time it (a tick's parts, as on 2026-11-10: `ARCHITECTURE.md`, performance budget), and keep the bench's figures honest: its character must stay alive (a dead one no longer keeps the world awake).
- **Generation stays order-free:** every tile and edge of a patch is a pure function of the seed, the layout and its place (`Layout::tile`, `north_edge`, `west_edge`), never of other chunks. `dusk-gen`'s tests check it.
- **Leave `11_PPO-Zombie` alone.** Don't edit it from here. Copy what's reused (`ppo.py`, telemetry, the dashboard) and note where it came from.
- **The client:**
  - **Rules live in the core.** A rule the client draws (sight, noise) comes from a core function (`World::sees`), never a GDScript copy of it.
  - **Generated files:** `client/dusk.gdextension` is written by `play.cmd` (it names this PC's build folder) and is gitignored, like `client/.godot/`. Commit the `.uid` files Godot makes next to scripts and shaders.
  - **Close the game before `cargo build`:** a running game holds the DLL. The editor loads a copy, so it reloads on a rebuild.
  - **Checking the look from the Bash tool:**
    - `cmd //c ".\\play.cmd --headless --quit-after 300 -- --news"` runs the scripts with no window, but it can't compile shaders. Keep a flag after `--`: without one it's the player's own game, and it loads and saves their world.
    - `cmd //c ".\\play.cmd -- --shot=<scratchpad>/x.png --shot-after=5"` opens the game for a few seconds on the user's desktop and saves a picture.
    - **Something timed** (the Tolling, a death): copy `data/` to the scratchpad, change the copy's numbers (`[clock] start`, a region's `[tolling] first` and `hour`, a need's `rise` and `harm`), and run with `--data=<copy>`. Headless runs about 300 frames a second, so `--quit-after` is in frames and `--do=S:` in seconds of play; a need's harm comes on the game hour.
  - **Colours:** client colours are picked in sRGB. MultiMesh instance colours go through `srgb_to_linear()`.
